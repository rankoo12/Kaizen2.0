But the headline promise â€” "only green survivors are proposed" â€” is delivering exclusively false confidence today: **all 4 validated-green authenticated drafts carry oracles that cannot fail** (one asserts the text it typed into the same input, `input.field`; three anchor "no-results message" assertions on the always-visible File menubar button), and the selection pressure is inverted â€” the one sharp oracle in the corpus (`assert_url "/prod.html"`) failed honestly and was rejected while vacuous oracles sailed through.
