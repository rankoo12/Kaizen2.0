This is OWASP Juice Shop — an Angular single-page web shop. On first load a
welcome banner and a cookie notice cover the page; every test must dismiss
them first (the "Dismiss" button and the "Me want it!" cookie button) before
doing anything else.

Write focused tests for these flows, one flow per test:
- Search: open the search (magnifier icon), type "juice", press Enter, verify
  a product with "Juice" in its name is listed.
- Product detail: click a product card, verify its description text shows in
  the dialog, close the dialog.
- Login failure: go to /#/login, submit a wrong email/password, verify the
  "Invalid email or password." error.
- Navigation: open the side menu (burger icon), verify "Customer Feedback" is
  a visible menu entry.

Keep tests 4-8 steps. The app renders slowly after navigation — assert on
text the page genuinely shows. Do not attempt registration or payment flows.
