PURPOSE
Nimbus Store is a synthetic single-page shop built with React. Three views on
hash routes: Home (#/), Products (#/products), Account (#/account). The page
renders client-side a moment after load; navigation never reloads the page.

ROLES
Anonymous visitor everywhere. The Account view "signs in" with any email —
it only shows a confirmation message; there is no real account.

CRITICAL FLOWS
- Home: headline "Gear that keeps up with you" and a "Browse products" button
  that opens the Products view.
- Products: four product cards — Aurora Lamp $49, Drift Keyboard $129, Pebble
  Mouse $39, Haven Headphones $199 — each with an "Add to wishlist" button.
  Clicking one shows "Added <product> to your wishlist."
- Account: an "Email address" field and a "Sign in" button. Clicking it shows
  "Signed in as <email>." (or "Signed in as guest." when the field is empty).
- The top nav (Home / Products / Account) switches views from anywhere.

BUSINESS RULES
- Nothing persists; every view resets on reload.
- The wishlist message always names the most recently added product.

PRIORITIES
Each view's primary action with its visible outcome: browsing from Home,
adding at least two different products to the wishlist, signing in with a
typed email, and nav switching between all three views.

TEST-WRITING STANDARD
- Name every test after its view and primary action.
- Every test must END on a step verifying the visible outcome text quoted
  above — never stop at the click.
- Keep tests short: navigate, act, verify.

CAUTIONS
- The app renders shortly after page load — nothing exists instantly.
- There is nothing to delete or configure anywhere.
- All data is synthetic; any typed values are safe.
