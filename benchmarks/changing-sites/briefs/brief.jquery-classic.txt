PURPOSE
Harbor Desk is a synthetic old-school support console: one page
(/index.html) with three tabs, an accordion of common questions, an
announcement modal, and an on-demand activity table.

ROLES
Anonymous agent; no login; nothing persists.

CRITICAL FLOWS
- Tabs: Overview (default), Reports, Activity. Clicking a tab shows its panel.
- Overview: the text "Queue is quiet: 3 open tickets" is visible, plus an
  "Open announcement" button that opens a "Scheduled maintenance" dialog with
  a "Got it" button that closes it.
- Common questions accordion on Overview: "Support hours", "Refund policy",
  "Escalation path". Clicking a heading reveals its answer text (e.g. Refund
  policy → "Full refund inside 30 days, no questions asked.").
- Reports tab: a "Load weekly report" button; clicking it shows
  "Weekly report ready: 42 tickets, 94% satisfaction."
- Activity tab: a "Load entries" button; clicking it shows a three-row table
  including "closed ticket #4411".

BUSINESS RULES
- Only one accordion answer is open at a time.
- The report line and activity rows do not exist until their buttons are
  clicked.

PRIORITIES
Each tab opening; the announcement modal opening AND closing; each accordion
answer; the weekly report line; the activity table contents.

TEST-WRITING STANDARD
- Name every test after its area and primary action.
- Every test must END on a step verifying the visible outcome text quoted
  above — never stop at the click.
- Keep tests short: act, verify.

CAUTIONS
- There is nothing to delete or configure anywhere.
- All content is synthetic and static; everything is safe to click.
