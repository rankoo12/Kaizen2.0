PURPOSE
Relay Ops is a synthetic delivery-operations dashboard that renders SLOWLY on
purpose: skeleton placeholders first, data when the fake backend answers.
Two pages: Dashboard (/index.html) and Deliveries (/deliveries.html).

ROLES
Anonymous operator; no login; nothing persists.

CRITICAL FLOWS
- Dashboard: three KPI cards appear after a short loading shimmer — "Open
  deliveries 12", "On-time rate 96%", "Fleet in service 8" — along with a
  "Refresh stats" button. Clicking it shows "Stats refreshed just now."
- Deliveries: a list of 20 rows streams in ("20 deliveries shown" when done),
  each like "Delivery #1001 — Ben · Osaka". A "Search deliveries" field
  filters live by courier or city (typing "Lisbon" shows 5, clearing shows
  20, with the count line updating). Clicking a row opens a detail panel
  titled "Delivery #<id>" with the courier, city and status.

BUSINESS RULES
- Everything renders late; content that is not there yet is loading, not
  missing.
- The "<n> deliveries shown" line always matches the visible rows.

PRIORITIES
The three KPI values; the refresh confirmation; the full list count; search
filtering with the count verified; a row's detail panel contents.

TEST-WRITING STANDARD
- Name every test after its page and primary action.
- Every test must END on a step verifying the visible outcome text quoted
  above — never stop at the click.
- Keep tests short: navigate, act, verify.

CAUTIONS
- Rendering is deliberately delayed — wait for content rather than declaring
  it absent.
- There is nothing to delete or configure anywhere; everything is synthetic.
