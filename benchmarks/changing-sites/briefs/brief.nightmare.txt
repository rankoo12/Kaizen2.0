This is "Blackout" — a deliberately hostile internal dashboard. Widgets load
slowly and at different speeds (some take several seconds); skeleton
placeholders render first. A cookie banner appears a few seconds after load
and must be accepted (the "Accept the inevitable" button) before interacting
with anything in the lower part of the page.

Write focused tests for these flows, one flow per test:
- Reactor: click the "Activate reactor" control, verify "Reactor online."
- Rating: click a star rating, verify the "Rated N stars" confirmation.
- Live feed: pin a feed row with its "Pin this" button, verify the
  "Pinned:" confirmation appears.
- Modal: click "Summon modal", type a short text, click "Seal it", verify the
  "Sealed:" confirmation shows the text.
- Ledger (#/ledger): verify the page shows ledger entries.
- About (#/about): verify the text "Built to break the brave."

Keep tests 3-6 steps. Widgets are SLOW — the content genuinely arrives, so
assert on the real outcome text. Never test the "Secret vault" or the
"I am not a robot" captcha — they are decorative.
