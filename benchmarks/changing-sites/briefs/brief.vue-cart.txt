PURPOSE
Verdant Grocer is a synthetic one-page grocery shop. Two views toggled by the
top nav — Catalog and Cart — with a live cart badge in the header.

ROLES
Anonymous shopper; no login; nothing persists past a reload.

CRITICAL FLOWS
- Catalog: four product cards — Curly Kale $2.50, Black Figs $5.00, Sourdough
  Loaf $6.20, Oat Milk $3.10 — each with an "Add to cart" button. Adding shows
  "<product> added to your cart." and the header badge shows the item count.
- Cart: lists added items, shows "In your cart: <n> items", and has a
  "Checkout" button. Checking out shows "Order placed — thank you!" and
  empties the cart. An empty cart shows "Your cart is empty — add something
  from the catalog."
- The nav's Catalog / Cart links switch views from anywhere.

BUSINESS RULES
- The badge always equals the number of items added and disappears at zero.
- Checkout only exists while the cart has items.

PRIORITIES
Adding one and several products with the confirmation and badge verified;
the cart listing what was added with the count line; checkout producing the
order confirmation; the empty-cart message.

TEST-WRITING STANDARD
- Name every test after its view and primary action.
- Every test must END on a step verifying the visible outcome text quoted
  above — never stop at the click.
- Keep tests short: act, verify.

CAUTIONS
- The page renders client-side a moment after load.
- There is nothing to delete or configure; checkout is fake and safe.
- All data is synthetic; any interaction is safe.
